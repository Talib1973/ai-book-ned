## What is Physical AI?

**Physical AI** refers to artificial intelligence systems that interact with and operate in the physical world. Unlike purely digital AI (like chatbots or recommendation systems), Physical AI must:

- **Perceive** the real world through sensors
- **Reason** about 3D space, physics, and dynamics
- **Act** through motors, actuators, and manipulators
- **Learn** from physical interactions and experiences

## Why Humanoid Robots?

Humanoid robots - robots designed to resemble human form and movement - are particularly exciting because:

1. **Human-Centric Environments** - Our world is built for humans, so humanoid forms can navigate existing infrastructure
2. **Natural Interaction** - Humanoid appearance facilitates intuitive human-robot interaction
3. **Versatility** - A general-purpose form factor can perform diverse tasks
4. **Research Value** - Understanding humanoid robotics helps us understand human intelligence and movement
